## The idea

Meaningful commits already tell a story. The Telegram bot turns that trail into draft writing — CV bullets, LinkedIn copy, or a short blog post.

## Landing in the portfolio

Approved blog drafts are posted here as Markdown under `src/content/blog/`. Astro picks them up on the next deploy, so `/blog` stays the single public reading surface.

## What to write next

Keep posts short, concrete, and tied to something you actually shipped.
