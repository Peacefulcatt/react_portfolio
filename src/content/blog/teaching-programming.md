Teaching forces precision. If you cannot explain a concept simply, you probably do not understand it deeply enough yet.

## What Teaching Gives Back

When I walk someone through a problem, I rediscover the fundamentals: naming, feedback loops, and the difference between working code and clear code.

## Habits That Stick

- Explain the *why* before the *how*
- Prefer small demos over long lectures
- Celebrate debugging as a skill, not a failure

## Closing Thought

The best engineers I know teach — formally or informally. Sharing knowledge compounds for everyone involved.
